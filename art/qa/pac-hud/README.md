Desktop folders correspond to 1280×720 and 1920×1080.

- `pac-hud.png`: persistent compact synergies/captains and six shop slots.
- `synergy-hover.png`: actual tiers, crew portraits and board highlights.
- `item-recipe-hover.png`: primary component card and one nested recipe card.
- `galley-card.png`: keyboard-opened Food card and original serving controls.
- `inspector-fallback.png`: independent crew inspector, inventory and explicit
  image-decode fallback from the unchanged B12 assertion.

three existing Foods. Captures are presentation evidence, not balance data.
